I am a first-year PhD student at NTU Singapore, supervised by Professor Stefano V. Albrecht in the Autonomous Agents Research Group (AARG). I began my research journey because I want to build a swarm of agents that can autonomously carry out useful tasks for humanity for months. Each project I work on brings me a little closer to that goal. I believe two of the central open challenges are multi-agent goal-driven learning and long-term memory. These are the challenges I am currently focused on.

I organize our group's RL reading sessions. If you are interested in presenting your work, please email me.
